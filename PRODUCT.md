# Macbook flip

## Product

A Chinese-language buying and resale analysis tool for used MacBook Pro listings from 闲鱼 and Carousell. The main job is to compare asking price, suggested purchase price and downside profit, inspect original photos, and retain personal analysis across devices.

## Users

An individual trader working on a Mac and an iPhone, often reviewing many listings in one sitting. Readable financial comparisons, dependable list position, explicit photo controls and low-friction cloud access matter more than decorative presentation.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the approved green background and simple white, notched MacBook logo. Round corners slightly; do not turn the laptop into an ellipse.
- Calm, minimal, professional, detailed when opened. Use Chinese operational language.
- Preserve real listing text, images, sellers, configuration, condition, battery, warranty and source links.

## Non-negotiable behavior

- Responsive aligned grid, two-line listing titles, profit-first default; four sort modes; saved platform filter.
- Live CNY/SGD rates, click-to-switch amounts; suggested buy price never exceeds asking price.
- 闲鱼: ¥150 shipping and ¥54 inspection. Carousell: neither charge. Show individual costs.
- Opening detail retains list position for return. Sorting scrolls to the top. Seller click copies only the name.
- Whole, uncropped photos; up to ten supplied photos; explicit centered previous/next buttons. Original-image viewer has buttons and arrow keys, no swipe-to-change; preserve pinch zoom.
- Hourly/startup/visibility/manual sync. No age-based expiry.
- Confirm deletion, synchronize GitHub deletion/tombstone, and preserve personal analysis and cloud data.
- No visible add-product action. Editing remains available. Tokens stay outside public source and data.
- Mac App, website, favicon and home-screen icons share the identity.

## Surface mode

Main list, details, editing and image inspection are **Operate** surfaces. Prefer legibility, factual content, familiar native controls and quiet feedback. Do not introduce marketing heroes, made-up metrics, scroll effects or a new framework for a visual refinement.
